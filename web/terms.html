About the outfit suggestions

Cinch's suggestions are suggestions. They come from a scoring algorithm and, in part, from an AI model, and both are sometimes wrong. Tags can be mistaken, the weather forecast can be off, and an outfit the app is confident about can still be a bad idea for your day.

Nothing Cinch produces is professional advice of any kind — not styling advice for an occasion where it matters, not advice about what is suitable for a workplace, a climate, a religious or cultural setting, or a medical need. Use your own judgement, and look out of the window before you dress for the weather. You decide what you wear; Cinch does not.
