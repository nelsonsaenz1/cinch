Your closet, styled.

Photograph what you own. Cinch works out what goes with what — and picks outfits that suit the weather, your plans, and the way you actually dress.

Built on what you already own

Every suggestion is assembled from clothes in your wardrobe. Nothing is imagined, nothing is a stock photo, and nothing is something you have to go and buy first.

It understands a whole day

A client dinner at seven and drinks at ten are not the same dress code. Cinch finds the one outfit that carries you through both, and tells you the single piece to lose on the way.

It learns you

Every like, dislike and wear moves the model. After a handful of reactions it starts favouring the combinations you actually reach for — and it will show you, in plain English, what it thinks it has learned, so you can tell it when it is wrong.

Private by default

Cinch never reads your photo library — iOS hands it only the pictures you pick. Your closet is private to your account. There is no advertising and no tracking across other apps. Read the privacy policy.
