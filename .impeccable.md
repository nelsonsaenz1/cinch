# Impeccable — project design context

## Design Context

### Users

People who own plenty of clothes and still feel they have nothing to wear. They
photograph their wardrobe once, then open Cinch in two very specific moments:

1. **Morning, half-awake, standing in front of a wardrobe.** They want to be
   told what to wear, not given a tool to browse. Seconds matter.
2. **Before something that matters** — a dinner, an interview, a trip. Here they
   will happily spend two minutes and want to feel advised, not sold to.

Both moments happen in daylight, at home, holding a phone in one hand.

### Brand personality

**Worn-in, candid, precise.**

- *Worn-in* — this is about clothes they already own, with wear counts and
  history. Nothing aspirational, nothing they have to go and buy.
- *Candid* — it says "you own nothing formal enough for black tie" rather than
  inventing a tuxedo. The honesty is the product.
- *Precise* — the engine is exact about colour, warmth and formality. The
  interface should feel measured, not chatty.

It is a knowledgeable friend with better taste than you, not a shopping app and
not a magazine.

### Aesthetic direction

**Editorial-minimal with streetwear restraint. SSENSE meets Apple Weather.**

- **Light theme, always.** Derived from context, not preference: the core loop is
  photographing clothes and dressing in the morning, both daylight activities.
  A dark UI would fight the garment cutouts, which are the actual content.
- **Warm paper canvas** (`#FAF9F6`), never pure white. Garments supply the
  colour; the interface stays out of their way.
- **One confident accent** — ultramarine `#1B3BFF`. Deliberately not the
  terracotta/amber that models default to on "editorial" briefs, because that
  palette now reads as AI-generated.
- **Type does the work.** A didone display face against a utilitarian grotesque:
  magazine masthead over garment care label.
- **Tabular numerals everywhere** numbers appear — wear counts, temperatures,
  cost per wear.

**Anti-references:** Pinterest (busy, infinite), Depop (loud, chaotic), any
wellness app (soft gradients, rounded blobs, sans-serif whisper).

### Type

| Role | Face | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Bodoni Moda** | The didone is the fashion-magazine letterform. High contrast, printed-page authority. Used only large — its hairlines are fragile below ~28px. |
| Body / UI | **Archivo** | Condensed grotesque built for small sizes, with real tabular numerals. Reads like a spec sheet. |

Rejected as training-data reflexes: Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Newsreader,
Crimson, Playfair Display, Cormorant, Inter, DM Sans.

### Design principles

1. **The clothes are the content.** The UI contributes structure and silence,
   never colour that competes with a garment.
2. **Say the true thing.** Degraded states are labelled, gaps are admitted,
   fallbacks are disclosed. Never imply certainty the engine does not have.
3. **Instant, then better.** The local engine renders immediately; the model's
   prose replaces it when it lands. Never block on the network.
4. **Motion explains, never decorates.** Staggered entrances show hierarchy and
   assembly. No bounce, no elastic — real objects decelerate smoothly.
5. **One clear action per screen.** Secondary paths are present but quiet.
