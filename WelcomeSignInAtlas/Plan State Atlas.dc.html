Every mode carries its own empty, loading and error-with-retry rendering; each frame is annotated with the condition that triggers it.

Three layers, and the order is the argument: the brief (the only part that costs a model call), the day-parts asked for, then the whole closet underneath. P-13Packing — the brief’s four failures
consent
Cinch needs your go-ahead first.
Reading a destination is an AI feature, and AI processing is switched off for your account. Turn it on in Profile and the brief will come through. Your closet below is unaffected.
quota
You’ve used this month’s styling allowance.
Reading a destination draws on the same allowance as the stylist, and yours is spent for now. It comes back when the month rolls over — the capsule and your closet below don’t use it at all.
unavailable
The destination brief isn’t live yet.
This part of packing has not been switched on for your build. Everything below still works — the capsule and your closet are read from your own wardrobe, not from a server.
network
The brief didn’t come back.
Cinch couldn’t read this destination just now — a connection problem, not a change to your things. The capsule and your closet below are unaffected.
Try again
Each one sits where the brief would have been; the capsule and the closet render beneath all four. Only the network case gets a retry — the other three are answered somewhere else, and a button that cannot fix anything is worse than a sentence that says so.

04 · The control
One ink pill, sliding
Live — tap a segment
Week
Events
Packing
Track radius 12, inset 4 on every side, so the pill is 8 — one continuous corner system rather than a second rounded thing inside a rounded thing. The pill is drawn once and slides; segments never paint their own fill. Each segment measures itself on mount, so a fourth mode or unequal labels stay correct.
On first layout the pill is placed instantly — there is nothing to slide from, and animating in from a corner reads as a bug. After that it springs, snappy, with a selection haptic. Reduce Motion settles it without the travel. Mode content cross-fades at the fast duration: turning to a different section of the same magazine, not pushing a new screen.
