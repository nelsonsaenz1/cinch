03 · The row
A catalogue line, not a feed cell
Anatomy
Tailored trousers
Charcoal · The Row
SO Sam Okafor 4h ago
The well — {{ well }}pt square, tray-coloured, radius 8. Contents are fitted, never cropped: a cutout the ingest pipeline has scale-normalised must not be cropped back out of true.
The spec line — piece, capitalised to the approved register, then colour and label joined by a middot. Nothing else: no price, no wear count, no original photograph. The friend view publishes only these columns.
The attribution — 22pt avatar, the name, and the elapsed label ranged right. “You” for your own pieces, “A friend” when a profile won’t resolve.
The whole row is one target, 44pt minimum, and it leads to the friend’s closet rather than to a detail page you have no right to see.
When there is no render
Plate
Belt
Swatch
Glyph
Three fallbacks in order: the plate, then the tagger’s colour printed as an honest swatch with the piece name on it, then the garment glyph. The swatch caption flips to light on dark fills by the same luminance test the closet thumbnails use — two answers to “is this swatch dark?” in one app is how a caption ends up invisible on exactly one screen.
What this page will not do
No strangers. Every row comes from the mutual-friendship-gated view plus your own pieces. Public projections exist but require flags no screen sets.
No metrics. No likes, no counts, no follower numbers — nothing that turns a diary into a scoreboard.
No infinite scroll. The page ends, and it ends on “Your circle”.
No notifications. Nothing here badges the tab bar; the page is somewhere you go, not somewhere that summons you.
One standing link. Requests, blocks and the full friends list live on Friends. This page points there — from the foot of a full timeline and from under an empty one.
