The direction

Cinch is treated as a print lookbook that regenerates every morning — considered, quiet, confident. Not a dashboard, not a feed, not a shop. It only ever styles clothes you already own, and it never oversells.

The data is the point but kept editorial: cost-per-wear, colour harmony, and real usage read like a caption or a spec line under a photograph, never like fine print. Garment photos are styled placeholders — drop the real shots in later.
