Implementation handoff
Route & ownershipsrc/app/(tabs)/closet.tsx owns the screen. Grid, rails, chips, capacity band, selection bar and the add control are presentation; counts, ceilings and every mutation stay server-authoritative. FlashList v2 continues to virtualize the grid — the three-column dense layout is a numColumns and estimated-size change, not a new list library.Reuse vs changeReuse: FlashList v2, the existing garment cell and its signed-path loader, the existing missing-image fallback, the existing category/search query layer, the existing store/restore and delete mutations, the existing Superwall placement door. Change: grid density (2 → 3 columns, 2pt gutters, tray wells), rails above the grid, chips gain query-aware counts, capacity becomes a designed band, add becomes a persistent filled control.RailsMost worn is derived from wear data and renders only when that data exists. Favourite outfits is the private favourite-outfits rail from Page 13 — it is not a favourite-garment state and no garment shows a favourite marker here. Either rail is absent, never empty.Capacity truthFree 10 active / 30 total, Standard 150 / 450, Pro 1000 / 3000. The band renders active in oxblood and stored in clay on one track. The client displays and preflights; the server decides. Being at the active limit never disables Add piece — the add attempt meets the truthful wall.Two different wallsActive full with storage room offers Store pieces, because storing frees an active slot while keeping the piece in the total. Total storage full says storing cannot help and offers delete or a larger closet. The two states are worded and remedied differently on purpose.FiltersCategory chips are horizontally scrollable with query-aware counts; zero-count chips are hidden except All. Search composes with the active category and matches name, colour and material. Counts always describe the current query, never the whole closet.SelectionLong-press enters selection with a selection haptic. One, many, all and zero are real states. Select all / Deselect all, recategorise, store or restore as appropriate, and delete with confirmation. Destructive copy states what leaves Cinch and that the camera roll is untouched. Partial failure preserves the selection and is retryable.ImagesGarment images load from private signed paths. A missing, unloaded or unauthorized image renders the same neutral well, so nothing in the UI reveals whether a piece exists but is hidden.Privacy & RLSNo new schema, RPC, view or policy is expected: closet reads and mutations already run under the owner’s RLS. Nothing on this page is public — no public counts, no recipient expansion, no share of garment data. Favourite outfits stay owner-private and are never exposed to social surfaces.Analytics allowlistcloset_viewed{rails:enum none|worn|outfits|both}, closet_filter{kind:enum category|search|both}, closet_selection{size:enum one|many|all}, closet_bulk{action:enum recategorise|store|restore|delete, result:enum ok|failed}, closet_capacity_state{enum room|active_full|total_full}, closet_plans_door_opened. Forbidden in every payload: item IDs, item names, categories as free text, search text, image paths or URLs, counts of owned pieces, plan price, user identity.AccessibilityFocus order: masthead → capacity band as one announcement → filter chips as a tab-like group → grid, each cell labelled by garment name and category → Add piece → tab bar. Selection mode announces the count and re-announces on change. All targets ≥44pt, ≥56pt at AX sizes. At AX-XXXL the grid becomes one-column rows so cards are never crushed; type is never clamped.Motion & hapticsSelection haptic on entering selection and on each toggle; notification haptic only after a committed bulk write. Reduce Motion keeps the exact composition and replaces the grid cascade and sheet spring with a short cross-fade.TestsCeilings read from plans for all three tiers; storing lowers active and preserves total; total-full shows the different remedy; Add piece stays enabled at the active limit; zero-count chips hidden except All; counts are query-aware; search matches material; selection one/many/all/zero; recategorise, store, restore and delete commit or fail atomically; delete failure preserves selection; missing image renders the neutral fallback; no analytics payload carries identity or content; AX-XXXL renders one-column rows without clipping; 667pt keeps the add control clear of the tab bar; Reduce Motion preserves state.Fingerprint / OTAPresentation only: no new package, permission, vendor, entitlement, native module or config plugin. The Expo fingerprint is unchanged and this ships over the air on the existing binary.

Masthead Instrument Serif 32/1.0; eyebrow 12 at .1em uppercase; body Helvetica 13–15. Grid three columns with 2pt gutters, tile 128–150pt; rail wells 96–118pt at 13pt radius; chips 44pt tall at 13pt radius; capacity band 16pt radius with a 7pt track; add control 60pt circle, 20pt from the right, 100pt above the frame bottom so it clears the tab bar; sheets 28pt top radius.
Deviations One. The brief allows a grid reading 2.5–3 columns; this uses a true three-column grid with 2pt gutters rather than a peeking half column, because a partially cut third column reads as a horizontal scroll affordance the grid does not have, and it breaks the one-column AX reflow cleanly.
