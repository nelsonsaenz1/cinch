Manage membership — the app administers, the store sells
Two app-owned routes and one vendor document. /membership shows what an account has and how to change or end it. /membership-upgrade states the deltas honestly and hands off. Superwall 254200 owns every price, term and purchase — nothing in this atlas types a price, and no screen promises a trial that App Store Connect does not currently offer. Frames are 393×852pt, exported at 790×1708.
Page brief — binding, verbatim 30 · Manage membership — Redesign: the typographic masthead (shipped today) plus plan cards with tinted fills per tier and honest deltas; upgrade CTA filled. Keep: no prices typed into layouts (StoreKit/Superwall renders prices), no fake trial copy. Note: the paywall itself is a Superwall document (254200) — redesigning it happens in the Superwall editor, not in app code; flag desired changes separately and they can ship instantly without an OTA.

Implementation handoff
Boundary tableApp-owned /membership (src/app/membership.tsx) reads subscription state and renders management. App-owned /membership-upgrade (src/app/membership-upgrade.tsx) renders honest deltas and opens the placement. Vendor-owned Superwall document 254200, campaign 101571, placement membership_upgrade owns every product, localized StoreKit price, term, purchase and restore behaviour. Apple’s subscription list owns cancellation and billing change. /membership-thanks is the app-owned landing after the SDK reports success.
