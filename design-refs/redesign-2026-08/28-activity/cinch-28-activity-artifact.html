Implementation handoff
Routes & components/activity in src/app/activity.tsx, pushed from Daily Social and never a tab. Details at /activity/invitation/[id] and /activity/reaction/[id], keyed on the recipient-owned event id rather than the subject id — which is why a revoked item resolves to a bounded "No longer available" instead of a dead link. Presentation only: rows become avatar plus one designed sentence plus a warm unseen dot, and the shipped colophon stays.One timeline, two sourcesRecipient-materialized activity_events (group invitation, accepted, declined, joined, left, you left, Daily Social reaction) merge with legacy activity_feed() (friend request, borrow, event invite, returned piece, votes, compliment) in strict reverse chronology. One list, one entry dot, one grammar. No second inbox, no ranking, no recommendation, no engagement scoring. outfit.saved.shared is declared but disabled and is drawn nowhere.Recipient-onlyRLS is the boundary and the design never works around it. seen_at is visible to the recipient alone — no read receipts, no sender-side view, no public feed, and no public-profile fallback when an authorized actor identity is missing. An unavailable actor renders as "A Cinch member" with a neutral mark.Boolean entry onlyThe entry indicator is a warm unseen dot and nothing else: no badge number, row count, unread count, streak, score, or mark-all-read. Count inference is refused too — reactions arrive as one row each, never as "2 reactions", so the list length is the only number and it belongs to the reader. The one legitimate number on the page is three friends voting, which is a fact about the event board.Seen without disturbanceOpening the resolved page marks the current visit seen, but nothing reorders, dissolves or loses its New marking while the reader is still in the list. The transition happens between visits, not under the reader’s eyes.RetentionThirty days, enforced server-side. The colophon is exactly "PRIVATE TO YOU · KEPT 30 DAYS" and the month section is the oldest thing that can exist, because the server has already removed anything beyond the window.Page-27 vocabulary correctionExisting Activity Center strings use "circle" inconsistently. Page 27 set the binding customer vocabulary: private-group events say group. Every group row, detail and confirmation in this atlas says group. "Your circle" survives only where it genuinely means the mutual-friends relationship, never a private group. The privacy footer is rewritten to distinguish mutual-friend activity from private-group activity without widening access or implying public data. This is an exact cross-stack copy correction for implementation, not a design preference.Answer statesEvery answerable row keeps honest pending, success, failure, retry and — critically — the was-told consequence, because whether the other person learned something is exactly what the reader cannot otherwise see. "Not now" is private dismissal, not a decline: it tells nobody, leaves the request open, and undoes. Every failure states that nothing was sent.Dismiss & Clear AllDismissing one item is recipient-only and reversible for five seconds; the copy states the other person was not told and the underlying thing still exists. Edit mode is the visible equivalent of swipe and long-press so removal never requires a gesture. Clear All empties notices only — it declines no invitation, removes no reaction, changes no group, undoes no social action, and tells nobody.
