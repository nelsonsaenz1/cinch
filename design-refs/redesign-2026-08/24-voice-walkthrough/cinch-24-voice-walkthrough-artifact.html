Voice walkthrough — talk, then go through the list
Three spoken examples carry the invitation; a wide warm waveform carries the recording; one large draft card at a time carries the review. Forty-four states of speaking a closet into a list — nothing entering the wardrobe until the owner has been through every card. Frames are 393×852pt (667pt where stated), exported at 790×1708.

Implementation handoff
Routes & ownershipsrc/app/walkthrough/index.tsx (invitation, recorder, reading), review.tsx (procession) and ceiling.tsx keep their routes. Reuse without contract change: use-recorder.ts, use-transcription.ts, use-parse.ts, use-procession.ts, walkthrough-copy.ts, the existing queries/actions/ceiling helpers, and LevelMeter, ReadingBack, FailureScreen, GarmentCard, CorrectionSheet, CountStepper, ProcessionMasthead. Changed: presentation only — spoken examples promoted to the visual body, waveform widened and warmed, procession card enlarged, ceiling set as ruled arithmetic.Exact legal sentenceRendered verbatim, unshortened, immediately above Start recording and focusable in its own right: "The recording goes to the service that turns speech into text, then to the model that reads the list." No vendor is named anywhere, because the transcription service is a configurable Whisper-class endpoint behind the server — naming one in copy would be false the moment it is swapped.Permission timingNo microphone permission on mount, on the Add clothes chooser, or on entering the walkthrough. The disclosure is read, Start recording is tapped, and only then does the iOS prompt appear. Denial states plainly that nothing was recorded and keeps the photograph door open.Recorderexpo-audio, AAC .m4a, Pause / Resume / Done, live amplitude waveform, tabular elapsed time, a twelve-minute hard cap, and finalisation of the partial recording if the app backgrounds. Paused time is excluded from elapsed. Haptics are best-effort; the Recording / Paused label and the timer are the authoritative feedback. The waveform is aria-hidden and never the only indicator of state.Silence nudgesAfter prolonged low amplitude a factual rotating nudge may appear. It is additive, never a failure, and never infers or prints what was said — no transcript exists at this point.Reading stateServer transcription then parse, with no percentage, bar or spinner. A duration-derived headline prints only when the real value is finite. At twelve seconds a "Still working" notice is added beneath the existing copy rather than replacing it. Leaving the page does not cancel the work; returning resumes the same session.Failure taxonomyFive distinct branches, each truthful about what is safe. Microphone denied: nothing recorded, Settings or photograph. Silent audio: unrecoverable, a new take is required, and Try again is deliberately absent. Offline or transcriber unreachable: the same recording is retained and genuinely resendable, so Try again is honest. Unparsed transcript: the verbatim transcript is retained and surfaced for review. Unauthenticated: sign in and resume. No raw service error, status code or vendor metadata reaches the reader.Recording retentionThe recording stays on device through any retryable failure and is deleted per the existing success, abandon and reaper contracts. The design makes no claim of cloud permanence and no claim that a recording survives beyond those contracts.Heard statePrints only real draft and unmatched counts. The principle is verbatim: "Some of these will be wrong. Go through them one at a time — keep, fix or drop each card. Nothing enters your closet until you have been through the list." Unmatched phrases are counted, kept with the recording, and surfaced at the end.Procession semanticsThese are drafts, not closet members — which is why Drop is legitimate here and was not on Page 22. Keep confirms. Fix confirms corrected data in one action. Drop rejects the draft from this import, offers Undo, and never deletes the source recording or transcript. The correction sheet cancels without writing and preserves the owner’s words. Ambiguous plurals resolve through a 1–12 stepper, one card per item.Product-photo handoffLookup uses the extracted garment words against the licensed catalogue while the original utterance stays quoted and unmodified on the card. Nothing typed or spoken is sent to an open-web search.Capacity ceilingA fact, not an upsell: no plan name, price, paywall or fabricated outfit count. Default selection and a manual "Keep N for now" resolve through the same path with the same result. Excess pieces wait in Storage; nothing spoken is deleted. An over-limit choice disarms the action and names the number that would work.CommitAfter the last card, confirmed drafts commit atomically through the existing owner-scoped contracts. The control locks during the write so a second tap cannot double-commit, and the closet is untouched until that write returns.PrivacyAudio, transcripts and drafts are private and owner-scoped under existing RLS and storage rules. No identifier, path, signed URL, utterance, transcript fragment, exact wardrobe content or free text appears in analytics, logs, accessibility strings or error copy. The private image fallback is identical for missing, expired and unauthorised assets and never reveals which.Analytics allowlistExisting bounded buckets only: voice_step { step: invitation | recording | reading | heard | review | ceiling }, voice_result { step, outcome: ok | failed, reason: mic_denied | silent | offline | service | unparsed | unauthenticated }, voice_review_action { action: keep | fix | drop }, voice_counts { drafts, unmatched, kept } as integers. Forbidden everywhere: utterances, transcripts, item names or identifiers, image paths or URLs, exact wardrobe contents, free text, price, friend data, and raw server errors.AccessibilityFocus order per phase: back or exit → phase headline → factual body or source → current card and its facts → primary actions → safety or retry notice. The waveform is hidden as decorative; elapsed, paused and listening state are one polite live region that does not chatter. Sheets trap focus; pending operations block duplicate submission without trapping forever. Targets are ≥44pt, ≥56pt at AX sizes. Dynamic Type is never clamped — examples and card facts wrap in full and the block scrolls.MotionDefault: waveform bars track amplitude, cards settle in, confirmed cards leave with a short travel. Reduce Motion freezes travel to amplitude change and fade, removes the card fly-off, and changes no state, focus or announcement.TestsPermission requested only after Start recording and never on mount or chooser; exact legal sentence present and unmodified. Pause and resume produce correct elapsed arithmetic with paused time excluded; waveform reflects amplitude only; silence nudge is additive; backgrounding finalises and keeps the partial; the twelve-minute cap stops cleanly with the recording safe. Each failure branch renders distinctly, and Try again appears only on genuinely retryable branches. Recording retention and deletion follow the existing success, abandon and reaper contracts. Unmatched phrases are retained and surfaced. Keep, Fix, Drop and Undo never delete the source; Drop is import-scoped. Ambiguous quantity accepts 1–12 and produces one card per item; correction cancel writes nothing. Product-photo lookup uses extracted words and preserves the utterance. Ceiling default and manual selection resolve identically; over-limit disarms. No identifier or utterance in any analytics payload. 667pt, AX-XXXL, VoiceOver order and Reduce Motion all verified.BackendZero change expected: existing intake audio bucket, transcribe-intake, parse-closet, owner-scoped sessions and drafts, RLS and reaper contracts all cover this design. No new schema, RPC, grant, package, vendor, permission, data recipient, public surface or billing surface. Expo fingerprint unchanged; ships over the air on the current binary.
