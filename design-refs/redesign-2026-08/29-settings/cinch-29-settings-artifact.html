Settings — administration that states only what it knows
Forty-eight states of one long grouped page. Every shipped capability stays reachable; a row that cannot work is absent rather than disabled; an unknown number is omitted rather than printed as zero. Frames are 393×852pt, exported at 790×1708.
Refusals held in this design A failed subscription read is never drawn as Free No price, period, renewal intent or upgrade word in the membership row Unsupported capabilities are absent, not dead — and request no permission Switch state is drawn as ON/OFF, never colour alone Partial deletion never claims that nothing was deleted

Implementation handoff
Route & shell/settings at src/app/settings.tsx, pushed from Profile’s gear inside a form sheet; Back returns to Profile. Not a tab. One long scrolling column of grouped cards — every existing capability stays on this page and stays reachable.Row inventoryMembership (conditional) · Daily outfit styling (fact) · Show where to buy (toggle) · Privacy & data · Invite a friend (conditional) · Friends · Borrow requests · Blocked people · Notifications (conditional) · Restore purchases (conditional) · Help · Privacy Policy (external) · Sign out · Delete account · Update diagnostics · version/build mark. Nothing was dropped and nothing was invented.Conditional capability matrixMembership: rendered unless the subscription read confirms free. Invite: only once the profile username is read. Notifications: only when NOTIFICATION_SETTINGS_AVAILABLE is true. Restore: only when purchases are configured and available. In every case the row is absent rather than disabled — a control that cannot work is worse than no control, and an absent row requests nothing.Membership state machinereading → "Membership" / "Checking…" / no chevron. failed → "Membership can’t be confirmed" / "Nothing has changed and nothing is locked" / Retry / no chevron. trialing → "Cinch [tier]" / "Trial through [server date] · [plan active ceiling]" / chevron. paid → "Cinch Standard" or "Cinch Pro" / optional "Through [server period end] · [ceiling]" / chevron. confirmed free → no row. A missing or failed read is never rendered as free. All values are Supabase subscription truth; no StoreKit price, period, renewal intent, trial offer or upgrade word appears in the row. Tapping routes to /membership.Trial truthThe server-side trial is 14 days, granted once. Settings may print the server’s exact end date when one exists and may print nothing else about it — no countdown, no renewability, no eligibility claim, no second trial.Styling card"Daily outfit styling" is a non-interactive statement in plain ink, not a disabled switch, because it cannot be turned off. "Show where to buy" is the real control and draws the word ON or OFF beside the switch, so state never rests on colour alone. Writing locks the control; failure restores the prior position and says the setting is exactly as it was. Privacy & data routes to /privacy.People cardInvite is the shipped /invite route styled as a warm designed row — no new link, referral reward, contact upload or public directory. Borrow may truthfully say "N waiting" and Blocked shows a count only after the list is read; unknown counts are omitted, never printed as 0. These details are facts in the value column, not navigation badges, and there is no notification count anywhere on the page.Restore state machineavailable → row with explanatory detail. pending → "Checking with the App Store…" and locked. restored → named toast. none found → distinct notice saying the store answered and nothing changed. failed → notice naming a connection problem, not a purchase problem, with retry. unavailable → row absent.Sign outA confirmation sheet with "Stay signed in" and "Sign out", both in neutral fills. Signing out is reversible, so it is never dressed in destructive colour. The body states the closet, outfits and friends survive.Delete accountThe only critical colour on the page. The typed-DELETE card names the piece count only when it is actually loaded and names paid-membership truth only when the subscription is known — including that Apple bills it and deleting here does not cancel it.
