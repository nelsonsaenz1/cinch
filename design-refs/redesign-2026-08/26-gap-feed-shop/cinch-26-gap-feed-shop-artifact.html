Gap feed — the outfit you can't make yet
The gap is the product; shopping is subordinate and often absent. Each card leads with the owner's own pieces and a marked absence, then a sentence they can check. Forty-four states, including four different reasons the merchant tray is simply not there. Frames are 393×852pt (667pt where stated), exported at 790×1708.
Contracts held in this design Ranking is closet arithmetic · commission never orders anything Disclosure belongs to the row, never to a mixed tray No qualifier, preference off, signed out, outage — all one silence Outbound carries referral and product only — no identity, ever A closet that cannot accept a piece never leads with where to buy one

Implementation handoff
Routes & ownershipsrc/app/gap-feed.tsx (feed) and src/app/gap-feed/[kind].tsx (detail) keep their routes. Reuse without contract change: GapCard, GapShopFooter, ProductSheet, GapLimitFooter, ClosetFullSheet, useGapFeed, useShopMatch. Changed: presentation only — the card is led by imagery of the outfits that cannot yet be made, the merchant tray becomes a per-row structure, and the product sheet is set as a reading surface rather than a buy button with text above it.RankingGaps rank deterministically by outfits unlocked from the owner’s own closet, computed before any commerce call. Commission, merchant, price and catalogue data never enter ranking or tie-breaking, and the merchant lookup runs after the order is fixed so it cannot influence it. The card prints the base and the result, never a bare delta — a reader can check the sentence against their own closet.CopyHeadline "What your closet is missing" is fixed. The subtitle counts truthfully: One / Two / Three gaps, computed from what you own. Nothing claims wear history was consulted, because it was not. Ranks read Biggest gap, Second gap, Third gap.ImageryEvery plate is an already-authorized owner garment image. The missing kind is always a dashed clay well with its name — never a rendered or sourced product standing in for something owned. A missing or unusable owner image becomes a designed No photo well that does not imply the piece was lost.Silence contractFour distinct causes — no qualifying match, the Show where to buy preference off, signed-out commerce, and a provider or catalogue outage — all produce exactly the same result: no tray, no outage notice, no retry, no placeholder. The gap arithmetic renders complete and unchanged. An outage in a subordinate feature is not surfaced as the reader’s problem.Merchant rowsAt most three, ordered by fit to the computed gap. Each row is independently truthful: a confirmed joined affiliate programme reads Open with an adjacent Paid link and commission copy on its own sheet; a direct or unpaid merchant URL reads Open at [Merchant] with no affiliate marker and no commission claim. A single block-level disclosure over mixed rows is prohibited by construction — the disclosure is a property of the row.Merchant dataAllowlisted merchants only, using their provided or licensed image, title, floor price, currency, name and URL. Never invented: merchant, logo, product, exact price, discount, stock, scarcity, countdown, sale badge, strike-through, deal language or paid placement. Price copy is a truthful floor ("from $88") and is omitted entirely when unknown — no dash, no From —, no estimate.Product sheetA merchant row opens an in-app single-product sheet, never the browser directly. The sheet names the merchant as the source of the picture and price, states that catalogue information can change, explains why this was suggested from the gap arithmetic, and — on a paid sheet only — states in full that Cinch may earn a small commission and that this does not change the gap, the match or the order. Only then is the external action offered. Purchase actions use ink, not oxblood. Back to the gap is always present. Dismissal buys nothing and changes nothing.Outbound privacyThe external click carries only the Cinch referral and the product. No account identifier, closet contents, friend graph, email or other identity leaves with it. No affiliate link appears in email, push, sharing, owned-garment detail, Today, Reveal, a plate, the diary, search or the closet grid — commerce is confined to this route and its sheet.CapacityActive and total are distinct ceilings with distinct remedies, read from plans.ts and never hard-coded. Active-full replaces the merchant tray with honest recovery and says storing makes room. Total-full replaces it with copy that says storing will not help, because stored pieces still count toward the total. When both are hit, total wins — it is the ceiling storing cannot solve. A screen that cannot accept a new piece never leads with where to buy one.Detail & paywallDetail shows up to three outfit cores from owned pieces with the absence marked. With room: "There’s room in your closet for this one." plus Add clothes. At active capacity: the real plan limit and See plans in the shipped position, opening the existing two-route ClosetFullSheet — See plans or swap a piece out, with cancel changing nothing. Superwall placement behaviour is preserved and no paywall is drawn here.Analytics allowlistBounded enums and counts only: gap_feed_view { gaps: 1|2|3 }, gap_open { rank: 1|2|3 }, shop_tray_shown { rows: 1|2|3 }, shop_row_open { paid: true|false }, shop_sheet_view { paid }, shop_outbound { paid }, capacity_block { kind: active|total }. Forbidden in every payload: account identifier, garment name or identifier, closet contents, friend data, image path or URL, merchant product identifier, exact price, free text, and any raw provider error.AccessibilityVoiceOver order per card: rank → missing kind → the gap sentence → owned pieces as one group → merchant rows, each read as title, merchant and price, Paid link where it applies, then the action. The gap reason is always announced before merchant content. Targets are ≥44pt, ≥56pt at AX sizes. Dynamic Type reflows and the disclosures scale with the body — the Paid link mark is never allowed to shrink relative to the row.MotionDefault: gap cards settle in with a short stagger and the tray fades in beneath its card. Reduce Motion removes the stagger and the fade travel; every state, disclosure and announcement is unchanged.TestsRanking is deterministic and independent of merchant data; injecting commission does not reorder. Subtitle count matches rendered gaps. Card prints base and result, never a bare delta. All four silence causes render identically with no tray artefact. At most three rows; per-row disclosure correct for paid and direct; a mixed tray never emits a block-level Paid link. Unknown price omits the price node. A match with an unusable image is suppressed rather than rendered broken. The sheet precedes any external navigation; the outbound URL contains no identity parameter; no affiliate link appears on any other surface. Active-full, total-full and both-full each render their own remedy and both-full resolves to total. Plan limits come from plans.ts. Paywall present, cancel and swap all leave the closet unchanged unless the owner acts. AX-XXXL, 667pt, VoiceOver order, Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast all verified.Deviation — broken product imageThe requested "merchant row with unavailable product image" is structurally impossible against current truth: useShopMatch drops a match whose licensed image is unusable before it reaches the tray, because rendering a broken or placeholder thumbnail would misrepresent a licensed catalogue asset. Frame 30 therefore documents that suppression — two rows where there would have been three, with no gap, apology or placeholder — rather than inventing the broken state.BackendZero change expected: existing gap computation, allowlisted merchant match, owner-scoped closet reads and RLS all cover this design. No new schema, RPC, grant, package, vendor, permission, data recipient, public surface or native module. OTA-compatible JS only; Expo fingerprint unchanged.
