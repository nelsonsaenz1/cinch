Item detail — one garment's life
src/app/item/[id].tsx. Hero cutout on paper, a designed stats band, pairings as an image rail, maintenance and lending as compact rows. Price is user-entered only; pairings prefer real co-wear; signed image URLs never leave the renderer. 36 frames at 393×852pt, exported at 790×1708.

Implementation handoff
Route & ownershipsrc/app/item/[id].tsx owns the screen. Hero, stats band, pairing rail, maintenance and lending rows are sections within it; the edit, price, store and delete sheets are presented from it. No new route, screen or navigator.Reuse vs changeReused unchanged: GarmentCell derivatives, the shared private image fallback, the existing lending switch and its invite door, storage/delete mutations and their confirmations, the manual tagging editor. Changed: presentation only — larger hero on paper, a designed stats band, pairings promoted to an image rail, maintenance and lending compacted into rows beneath.PriceUser-entered only, and only ever typed by the owner. There is no estimate, lookup, scrape, inference or default. Absent price means the cost cell does not render — not blank, not dashed, not zero. Never-worn means no cost per wear regardless of price, because the divisor is zero.PairingsReal co-wear history first, each entry carrying how often the two were logged together. Colour harmony renders only when co-wear is unavailable, under a renamed label and an explicit qualifier. Both draw only from garments the owner actually owns; nothing is invented to fill the rail.ImagesHero prefers the private signed listing image, falling back to the owner photograph, then the shared private fallback. Signed URLs and storage paths are bearer credentials: never rendered as text, never logged, never placed in analytics or accessibility strings. The fallback is identical for missing, expired and unauthorized so no state is inferable.Listing sourceA colophon at the foot of the hero, not a badge and never a merchant card. Removing the listing photo restores the owner photograph and is reversible by searching again.LendingPrivate and friend-scoped. The switch changes who may request the piece, never public visibility. Failure copy is always about the write, never about another person — no blocks, mutes, reachability or account state is ever revealed.Storage & deleteStorage is reversible and keeps wear history. Delete forever is destructive, names the number of wears that go with it, offers storing inside the same sheet, locks during the write, and on failure states that nothing was partly removed.TaggingThe source photograph stays visible for the whole editor. Cancel and failure both preserve typed work; nothing is discarded silently.AnalyticsAllowlisted buckets only: item_detail_viewed, price_added(bool), lending_set(bool), maintenance_action(enum), listing_photo_removed(bool), item_stored(bool), item_deleted(bool), action_failed(cause enum). Forbidden in every payload: item ID, item name, brand, price value, image path or URL, tags, colours, wear counts, free text, friend identity.Privacy & RLSNo new table, column, RPC, policy or grant. Reads and writes run under the existing owner-scoped policies. Nothing on this page is public, and nothing here enters any social payload.AccessibilityFocus order: back → edit → hero as one labelled group → name and category → stats band as one announcement per fact → pairing rail (label, qualifier where present, then each item) → maintenance rows → lending switch with state and private explanation → destructive row. Sheets trap focus, announce their title, and lock every dismissal while a write is open. All targets ≥44pt, ≥56pt at AX sizes.Dynamic TypeAt AX-XXXL the hero yields height, the stats band becomes ruled label-left/figure-right rows, and the rail moves below the fold in normal scroll. Text never shrinks, never clamps, and descenders never clip.Reduce MotionHero settle, rail stagger and sheet spring are replaced by a short cross-fade. Composition, state and feedback semantics are unchanged.TestsUnit: cost per wear omitted when price is null or wears are zero; colour rail only when co-wear is empty and always labelled; fallback identical across missing/expired/unauthorized. Integration: lending failure leaves the switch unchanged; edit failure preserves fields; store is reversible; delete confirmation blocks double submit and failure leaves the row intact. Privacy: no signed URL in any log, analytics payload or accessibility string. Simulator: standard, AX-XXXL, 667pt, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver order.Fingerprint / OTAPresentation only — no package, permission, vendor, recipient, public surface or billing added. Expo fingerprint unchanged; ships by compatible OTA after tests.DeviationsOne: the co-wear rail is a two-column grid rather than a horizontal scroller, so every pairing and its frequency is readable without a swipe at any type size. Documented rather than silently applied.
