02 · Anatomy
What the card is allowed to say
The five bands, top to bottom
Colophon rail — wordmark left, date right, both at 26pt on the 1080 canvas. It reads at thumbnail size, which is the only size most people will see.
The name — Instrument Serif at 136pt, two lines maximum. Longer names shrink to 112pt rather than wrap to three.
The stack — five garments at most, sized by role: outerwear largest, accessories smallest, staggered so no two edges align.
The list — the same piece names the plate shows, in the same order, so the card and the app never disagree.
The signature — palette dots and weather on the left, Cinch on the right. The one place the app advertises itself.
Rules the render enforces
No weather, no colophon. weather === null removes the line; it is never estimated.
No missing photographs. A garment without a plate falls back to its swatch, same as everywhere else — the card never ships an empty tile.
No name, no card. An unnamed outfit cannot be shared; the share action stays disabled until the stylist has named it.
No price. Cost-per-wear is yours. It appears on the plate in the app and nowhere on the artifact that leaves the phone.
No faces, no location, no handle. Nothing on the card identifies who made it.
Failure
Quiet authority

The render happens before the sheet opens, so a failure never shows an empty share sheet. The plate keeps its own state, the message sits under the name, and the sheet simply does not appear.
