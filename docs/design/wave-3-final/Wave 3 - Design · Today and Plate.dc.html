Wave 3 - Design Atlas group 2 of 6 · Today and the daily plate
The outfit arrives before anything else does
Four corrections drive this group. The greeting stops consuming the first viewport. The temperature is printed once. The plate stops representing garments as overlapping colour rectangles and shows the owned photographs instead. And the three reactions stay legible, in order, at the largest supported type.
Cost-per-wear is printed only when every piece in the look carries a price; otherwise the line is dropped, never estimated and never rendered as a dash. The weekly figure is PLANS.free.outfitPromise — the promise, never the enforced ceiling.

08 · Accessibility behaviour
One outfit, one summary, three decisions
VoiceOver
Reading order is context label, greeting, plate, actions, tab bar. The plate’s garment grid is one node: “Outfit, Quiet authority, five pieces: mohair sweater, tailored trousers, penny loafers, shearling coat, leather tote.” Individual tiles are reachable by swiping into the group, each labelled with its garment name and the hint “opens the piece”.
The palette announces its names, never its hex values. Cost-per-wear announces as “two dollars fourteen a wear”; when it is absent nothing is announced in its place.
Reactions announce their state as part of the label: “Loved, selected”, “Not for me, button”. A failure is a polite live region on the band, and the retry is the next focusable element after the row that failed.
Targets, contrast, second channels
The reaction row is the group’s tightest arrangement: three controls at 48 pt with 8 pt gaps, the wear action given 1.4× the width of the others so the dominant action is dominant by size as well as fill. At the largest type they stack at 60 pt.
A selected reaction carries a fill, a tick and a changed verb. A failed reaction carries an outline in oxblood plus the words “Not saved”. A disabled reaction is greyed and accompanied by a sentence naming the reason — never greyed alone.
Garment tiles carry a 1 px hairline at 9% ink so a pale garment on the warm plate ground still reads as a bounded object at low contrast.
Motion and type
Today has no bouncy moment. The plate cross-fades in at 140 ms; the swap sheet rises at 380 ms; the reaction row replaces itself at 220 ms with no spring. Under Reduce Motion the skeleton shimmer stops and every transition becomes an opacity change.
At the largest type the greeting is the first thing dropped, then disabled controls, then the rationale scrolls. Nothing is ellipsised and no scale is capped; the garment list wraps to as many lines as it needs.
There is no keyboard surface in this group. The diary note sheet, reached from T-14, carries its own and is drawn in group 4.

Correction 08 · T-12
Resolved: “Build one anyway” is deleted
A refusal that can be overridden is not a refusal, and the engine has no below-threshold mode to fall back to. The action is removed; the two honest recoveries remain — look at what you wore in comparable weather, or see what the closet is missing — and both lead somewhere real. Superseded text: The refusal offers to build a look regardless. Whether that returns the best available compromise with a caveat on the plate, or the same refusal a second time, is a behaviour question the shipping engine has to answer before the destination frame can be drawn honestly.
Claim card retired; the correction ledger in group 6 carries it.
Resolved from the codebase
The free weekly figure is three, from outfitPromise, and is the promise rather than the enforced five — so “1 of 3 this week” is safe to print.
