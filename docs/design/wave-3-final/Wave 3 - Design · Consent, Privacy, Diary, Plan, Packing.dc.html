Three separate things happen when Cinch suggests something to buy, and they are not the same.
Searching listings
Cinch sends the words describing the gap — for example “navy wool crewneck” — to a licensed product-listing service. Your identity, closet and friends are not sent.

Your sentence itself is discarded. What can remain is a scrambled lookup key and an unordered set of up to twelve generic garment words — navy, wool, crewneck — so the same search does not have to be repeated. The set carries no order and nothing personal.

Product images are fetched from the retailer, which means your device asks the retailer’s server for a picture. That request carries no Cinch account information.

08 · Accessibility behaviour
Legal text that survives larger type
VoiceOver
A consent fact reads as heading then sentence, so a reader can jump between the four by heading. The sheet announces “Reading your photographs, four things to know, three actions” on open, which is the five-second summary the acceptance line asks for.
A disclosure row announces title, gist and “collapsed” or “expanded”; the AI row adds its live state and count. Expanding moves focus to the section body, not back to the top of the page.
A diary row is one element: “Thursday 7 August, Quiet authority with the cognac shearling coat, outfit of five pieces, two dollars fourteen a wear”. A planned row begins “Planned, not worn yet” before the name.
A packed piece announces its count as words — “Linen blouse, worn on three days” — and the bag exposes a summary, “nine pieces for six nights”, rather than nine separate image nodes.
Second channels and targets
Selected terms and multi-select answers carry ring, tick and Medium weight. A planned diary row carries a word, a weight change and a rule. Toggle state is printed as On or Off beside the switch, never left to the switch alone.
Every action is at least 44 pt, including the text exits Not now, Keep it off, Plan this day and Share. Actions inside notice trays are 44 pt with 8 pt separation.
Body text stays 17 pt in every expanded privacy section and every consent fact. No legal sentence is set below 15 pt anywhere in the group, and none uses Medium.
Skeletons in PL-03 mirror the day row exactly. The memo’s single bouncy settle is suppressed under Reduce Motion; expansion becomes an instant height change rather than an animated one.
Largest Dynamic Type
Two stress frames carry the group’s two hard cases. CN-01 / AX scrolls the facts and pins the three actions, so the sheet still never covers its own footer. PK-01 / AX gives each term row two lines and pins the CTA with its tier and figure intact.
Elsewhere the pattern is the same: the diary row’s name takes a third line, the plan day row stacks its thumbnails under its type rather than beside it, and the packed grid drops from three columns to two. Height is allowed; nothing is truncated.
Where a cut is unavoidable it is stated in words — “two more facts below” — so no reader has to discover that content continues.
