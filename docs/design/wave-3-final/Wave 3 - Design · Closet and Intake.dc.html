Wave 3 - Design Atlas group 3 of 6 · Closet, garment intake, and review
The wardrobe, and the four doors that fill it
Thirteen corrections across six surfaces, drawn as one continuous intake path: the grid a returning user scans, one garment’s ownership story, the chooser, the camera, the words-or-URL field, and the card that confirms a guess. The metadata gets lighter, the garment gets larger, and the decision moves up to sit beside the thing being decided.
Category chips carry live counts from categoryCounts(items, q), which drops any category with nothing filed under it and keeps All even at zero. Cost-per-wear comes from costPerWear(), which returns nothing without both a price and a wear — so the line is present or absent, never zero and never a dash.

09 · Accessibility behaviour
Reading order, second channels, and larger type
VoiceOver
A garment card is one element, announced as “Mohair sweater, worn 14 times, six dollars forty-three a wear, photograph” — never as an image node followed by two unrelated labels. A silhouetted piece ends with “silhouette, no photograph yet”, which is a fact rather than a warning.
Chips announce label, count and state: “Tops, 41 pieces, selected”. The rail exposes its scroll extent so the partial chip at the edge is reachable by swipe as well as by touch.
The stat band reads label-then-figure per cell in visual order. The captured queue announces “three pieces filed, one ready to review” on change, once, not per tick.
Icon-only controls carry labels and hints: the shutter is “Take a photograph, files this piece and stays open”; the overflow is “More actions for this piece”; Undo in Review is “Undo, restores the last card you decided”.
Second channels and targets
Selection carries outline, tick and a word. Unavailability carries tray fill, a struck icon and a printed cause. Low confidence carries hedged wording. Nothing in this group is distinguished by colour alone, and no state is expressed by opacity on text.
Every control is at least 44 pt, including the text actions More details, Open Settings, Describe and Send it now. Chips are 38 pt tall inside a 44 pt row with 8 pt gaps, so hit regions do not overlap.
Keyboard states are drawn for Describe (C-02, DS-01): the field, the reason line and Find it all stay above the keyboard, and no error is ever placed behind it.
Skeletons mirror final geometry exactly in C-06 and DS-04; their breath holds at mid opacity under Reduce Motion, and the capture flash is suppressed entirely.
Largest Dynamic Type
Three stress frames are drawn: C-01 / AX turns the grid into one row per piece, IT-01 / AX turns the stat band into ruled rows and stacks the actions, RV-01 / AX grows the sheet to 780 pt and collapses three correction chips into one row.
In every case composition changes and height is allowed. Nothing is capped harder, truncated, ellipsised or scaled below its documented size, and no horizontal action row survives as a row when its labels no longer fit.
Camera chrome at the largest size grows its labels and keeps the shutter at 72 pt; the instruction sentence is allowed two lines and the foot grows to 156 pt, taking the space from the preview rather than from the type.

Claim 4 · DS-03
How the listing partner must be credited
Every result row names the retailer whose page the picture and details came from. Whether the licensed feed also requires naming the aggregator, and in what words, is set by the licence rather than by design.
Needed: the attribution clause of the current listing-search licence.
Correction 10 · CP-05
Resolved: the cutout is local, so nothing is queued
There is no retention window to define: Apple Vision runs on the device, so a failure is immediate and a retry is immediate. The frame keeps the full photograph as the piece’s image and offers to try again, with nothing pending. Superseded text: The frame promises the full photograph is used until a cutout succeeds, and offers a retry with no stated expiry. If the pipeline discards a source after a fixed window, the notice must say so before the window closes.
Claim card retired; the correction ledger in group 6 carries it.
Claim 6 · C-07
Whether stored pieces have their own ceiling
The plans define both an awake and a stored figure, and this group draws storing as the free recovery for a full shelf. What the screen should say when the stored count also reaches its limit is not yet defined.
Needed: the intended behaviour at the stored ceiling.
