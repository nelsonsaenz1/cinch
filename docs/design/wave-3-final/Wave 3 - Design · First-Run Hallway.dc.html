Wave 3 - Design Atlas group 1 of 6 · First-run hallway
The hallway, redrawn as one continuous surface
Seven connected screens between the first launch and a usable closet: Tour, the two-door choice, The Ten or the Walkthrough, the Procession, the Reveal, the dismissible paywall, and the Today the dismissal lands on. They share one door-plate geometry, one camera chrome, one count language and one transition rhythm, and they are drawn together here because a reviewer cannot judge whether photography and voice read as peers by looking at either one alone.
Every number printed in these frames is read from the shipping code, not typed into a layout. Ten named positions and thirty stored pieces come from PLANS.free; outfit totals come from countOutfits; slot copy comes from ten-copy.ts; the annual price hints from plans.ts. Where a fact does not exist in the codebase it is drawn as a [CLAIM] placeholder and listed in section 14.

11 · Accessibility behaviour
Order, labels, targets, motion
VoiceOver order and labels
Reading order follows the visual order on every frame: masthead count, then the subject, then the actions. The ten-tile plate on TR-01 and every composed-look grid is one node announcing a summary — “Outfit, Quiet authority, five pieces: mohair sweater, tailored trousers, penny loafers, shearling coat, leather tote” — rather than five unrelated image nodes.
Slot tiles are labelled by position and status: “Slot 3, top, empty, currently suggested”. Filled tiles name the garment. The level meter is not focusable; the recording state is announced once when it starts and the nudge is a polite live region that never interrupts.
Undo carries the hint “restores the last card you decided”. The paywall close is labelled “Close, keep the free ten” so its consequence is spoken, not only its shape.
Targets and second channels
Every action is at least 44×44 pt with 8 pt clear between neighbouring hit regions; nothing relies on invisible hit slop. The tightest row in the group is T-02’s Photograph / Library / Skip, drawn at 48 pt with 10 pt gaps, and it stacks at the largest type rather than compressing.
Selection is never colour alone: the recommended redirect carries a ring and the word “Recommended”, the selected term carries a ring and is named again in the CTA, and a missing kind on R-05 carries the word “needed” beside the figure.
Secondary text is #6B665C throughout. #8A8578 appears only as a placeholder glyph inside an empty tile, never as text a person is expected to read.
Motion and keyboard
The one bouncy moment in the hallway is the Reveal’s first outfit card settling in. Under Reduce Motion it fades in place at 140 ms. The level meter becomes a static mic-live glyph; card advances become cross-fades; the sheet rise becomes an opacity change.
Loading skeletons on V-04 mirror the procession card’s exact height and radius, so nothing shifts when real content arrives.
One keyboard surface exists in this group: the procession’s edit sheet. The active field, its error and the confirm action all stay above the keyboard, and the card being corrected stays visible above the field.

14 · Open claims and stop conditions
Three facts this group could not source
Space is reserved and the frame is drawn; the wording is not invented. Each needs a decision before implementation.
Claim 1 · TR-07
Model-provider retention
The consent sheet may only claim what Cinch controls. Whether the providers retain or train on submitted photographs is not settled by a frozen contract, so the sentence is drawn as a placeholder. Until it is, the sheet says what Cinch does with the image and stops there.
Needed: the frozen provider terms, or a decision to say nothing about retention at all.
Correction 05 · MB-07
Resolved: the frozen two-tier document, Pro preselected
First-run is Superwall document 254200, a two-tier sheet with Pro preselected — drawn here as MB-07. The one-tier alternatives are exploration only and are retired from the shipping handoff; MB-01 and MB-06 stay in this canvas as rejected treatments and no other surface routes to them. Superseded: Two tiers exist above free, and all three treatments are now drawn: Standard as the first-run default (MB-01), Pro on its own sheet (MB-06), and both on one sheet with Standard held (MB-07). Standard stays the drawn default, on the reasoning that a closet that has just met its tenth piece is being offered 150 rather than 1,000. If the placement is meant to sell Pro, the figures and the headline change; the layout does not.
Claim card retired; the correction ledger in group 6 carries it.
Correction 06 · MB-07
Resolved: no default trial is drawn
There is no hard-coded fourteen-day trial and no default trial of any length. An offer appears only when StoreKit’s runtime eligibility and the product’s own terms both prove one exists, in which case the term row states it in the store’s words. Every frame here is drawn in the no-offer case. Superseded: A trial-expiry placement exists in the code, which implies a trial somewhere, but no first-run trial length is defined. No trial language is drawn rather than guessing a duration, and the CTA names the charge directly.
Claim card retired; dependency E in the group 6 ledger records the StoreKit read this depends on.
