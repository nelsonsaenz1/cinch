08 · Accessibility behaviour
Settings that survive being read aloud
VoiceOver
A settled row announces label, then value or state, then its role: “Notifications, button”; “Show where to buy, on, switch”; “Blocked people, 3, button”. An overridden toggle adds “unavailable, your phone is not allowing notifications”. The membership row announces “Membership, checking” while the read is in flight and “Membership can’t be confirmed, retry, button” when it fails, so neither state is silent.
The unblock sheet announces its question, then all three consequence paragraphs, then the two actions — and the actions are last in the reading order as well as last visually, so nobody confirms before hearing the consequence.
Help titles are announced as the questions they are. The article’s headings are real headings, so the rotor can move between Turning them off and Your friends’ clothes.
The entitlement band reads label-then-value per row; the upgrade deltas read “active pieces, 150 becomes 1,000” rather than reading the arrow as a symbol.
Second channels and targets
Every toggle prints its state as a word beneath its label, so no preference is carried by the track colour alone. An overridden toggle carries tray fill, a paper knob, conditional wording and a stated cause — four channels.
Rows are at least 44 pt and grow to 52 at larger type; every trailing affordance, including Unblock, Retry and the sheet’s own Done, is a 44 pt target. The Morning edit time rail uses 38 pt chips inside a 44 pt row, and shows all four times — there is no partially revealed chip to mis-tap.
Nothing in this group animates except sheet entry, and no state in it depends on motion. There is no bouncy moment anywhere in group 6 — blocking, unblocking and paying are not delights.
The prose system never sets a factual sentence below 15 pt, and never uses Medium for one. Legal and support text is the same 17 pt as the rest of an article.
Largest Dynamic Type
Two stress frames. SF-02 / AX scrolls the consequence and pins both actions, because a safety decision must never have its options off screen. PR-01 / AX moves each value under its label and keeps toggles trailing with their state on a second line.
Help and the articles need no separate stress frame: the prose system is one column of wrapping paragraphs, which grows in height only. That is the reason help is prose rather than a set of cards.
MB-09 follows the group 4 pattern for term rows: each row takes two lines, the CTA pins with its tier and figure, and the entitlement deltas become label-over-value pairs.
