Wave 3 - Design Atlas group 5 of 6 · Gap Feed, friends, activity, occasions, boards
The gap is the product. Shopping is the footnote.
Fifteen corrections across eight social and commerce surfaces. The Gap Feed opens on a computed gap instead of a paragraph, and every gain figure says what it is a gain of. The social pages get their height back: compact rows, one strong action each, names carrying the emphasis instead of badges. Voting stops looking like a score, and captions stop truncating.
Commerce appears in exactly two places in the whole product — the Gap Feed recommendation and an opted-in friend’s piece. Disclosure is per merchant row: Paid link only where Cinch has a joined arrangement, Open at the merchant everywhere else. Commission never touches gap, match or order.
Revised against the repository review, corrections 19–27. The Look Board is a shared rail across attendees that locks itself at the occasion’s start; comments close on the same timestamp; Activity holds 30 days; and friends rows carry only real friendship data.

11 · Accessibility behaviour
Sentences, not stacks of badges
VoiceOver
A gap card announces its explanation before its merchants, and each row carries its own disclosure in its own label: “Everlane, wide-leg twill, from eighty-eight dollars, paid link, opens Everlane” and “Uniqlo, pleated trouser, from forty-nine dollars, opens Uniqlo”. Nothing announces a block-level marker, because none exists.
An activity row reads as its sentence, then its age, then its actions: “Sarah invited you to her birthday dinner on Friday, yesterday, Join, Later”. No row announces an unread state, because none exists.
A look board card announces whose look it is, then its caption, then a summary of its composition — “Sarah, four pieces: linen blouse, corduroy skirt, leather boots, crossbody bag” — then its count and action. Ahead is announced as “currently ahead”; a tie announces “even”; a locked board announces “closed, the evening has started” before its cards.
A quadrant plate is one element: “Linen and corduroy, three pieces, saved in June, worn twice”. The designed fourth cell is not announced as an image.
Second channels and targets
Selection in the picker carries an outline and a number. A placed pick carries blush, Medium weight and the words Your vote. Ahead, a tie and a lock are words. A pending action is a present participle. Nothing in the group depends on colour alone.
Every action is at least 44 pt: inline Accept and Join, the quiet Not now and Later beside them, Reply, the overflow controls beside comments, and the text actions in every tray.
The comment composer is drawn with the keyboard up in CM-01: reply context, draft and Send all sit above it, and a failure appears in the thread rather than behind the keys.
With the crown removed by correction 24, this group has no bouncy moment at all. The board lock, the vote move and every notice are base-220 state changes, which means nothing here needs a Reduce Motion exception.
Largest Dynamic Type
Two stress frames carry the group’s horizontal action rows. GF-02 / AX unstacks the merchant row into a card with a full-width Open and grows Paid link to 14 pt Medium. SL-01 / AX drops the picker to two columns and the bar to a count-plus-action stack at 138 pt, keeping an extra grid row visible.
The other rows follow documented patterns: a person row’s action moves under its name, an activity row’s inline pair becomes two stacked actions, and a look board card’s tiles drop to two columns of two. Captions and sentences take extra lines; nothing truncates.
The comment column is the one measurement that does not scale with type: it stays 261 pt and the comment simply grows taller, which is what keeps a three-line comment a stable rectangle at every size.

Dependency A · CM-01, CM-02
Server-side refusal of late comment inserts
The closed state tells a user that nothing new can be added. That is only true once the server refuses inserts after starts_at; a client-side hide would leave the rule unenforced and the copy false.
Until then, closure is presentational and the canvases overstate it.
Dependency B · LB-01, LB-02
Board lock at starts_at, one pick per viewer
The rail is drawn as locking itself when the occasion begins, and as holding exactly one movable pick per viewer. Both are server rules; neither is a client behaviour the canvases can guarantee on their own.
