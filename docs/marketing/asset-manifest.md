## Read first: three findings that change this list

**1 · Apple Ads is not the expensive channel. It's the only one that works at your budget.**

| Channel | Works at $33/day? | Learning phase | Est. CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apple Ads** | ✅ **No minimum budget, none stated anywhere in Apple's docs** | ✅ **None — a keyword auction has no model to train** | **$156** |
| Meta | ⚠️ Install-only, inside SKAN's noise band | ❌ ~50 conversion events/ad set/week. At $33/day you generate **~1 purchase/week** | $275 |
| TikTok | ❌ **$50/day campaign minimum — above your daily budget** | ❌ ~50/ad group/week | — |
| Google App Campaigns | ❌ Google's own doc: **daily budget = 50× target CPI** → $100–400/day | ❌ | — |

Your whole 3-month budget is roughly **five days** of Google's documented minimum. And on iOS non-gaming, Apple's CPI ($1.80–3.50) is *below* Meta's ($2.50–5.00) — the "Apple is expensive" premise doesn't survive contact with iOS-specific data. Apple installs also convert to paid at roughly **2× the rate** of social-targeted installs and retain 30–50% better, so Meta's cheaper click becomes a 76% more expensive customer.

**2 · Apple Ads creative cannot contain an AI avatar. At all.**

Apple builds ad variations *from your App Store Connect assets* — screenshots, App Previews, Custom Product Pages. And App Preview rules state: *"must show only content within the app itself. Don't film people interacting with a device, such as an over-the-shoulder angle or fingers tapping the screen."*

So the AI-UGC plan and the correct-channel plan are mutually exclusive. **This is good news** — it means your creative budget goes into App Store assets, which you need anyway, and Apple Ads costs you **zero incremental creative**.

**3 · The AI-testimonial idea carries a specific legal risk, and no tool does what you think.**

- **16 CFR § 465.2(a)(1)** (in force since Oct 2024) prohibits testimonials that misrepresent *"that the reviewer or testimonialist exists."* The FTC's own release names *"AI-generated fake reviews"* explicitly, and the rule unlocked civil penalties. An AI avatar saying *"I've used this for three months and my mornings are easier"* is a testimonial from a person who does not exist. **Demonstrative voice is fine** ("here's how it builds an outfit"); **first-person experiential claims are not.** Every template these tools ship is written in the prohibited register.
- **TikTok mandates AI disclosure as of 21 July 2026** and auto-detects it via C2PA content credentials — including on files made elsewhere.
- **No tool reliably composites an app screen recording into an AI avatar's phone.** Every "product in hand" feature (Creatify, MakeUGC, Topview) works from a *static product photo*. An app is a moving screen — a much harder tracked-replacement problem. You'd still be cutting to your own screen recording in CapCut. The AI replaces the actor, not the ad. If any vendor claims otherwise, demand a live demo with *your* recording before paying.
- The best independent evidence (Columbia/Harvard/TUM/CMU, 500M+ impressions) finds AI ads at **parity** with human ads after controls — not superior — and that parity holds only while the AI isn't detected. When identical ads are *labeled* AI, consumers rate them worse and click through less, with the effect strongest on **lifestyle** products.

**Verdict:** cap AI-video tool spend at **$150 across all three months**, use it in Month 3 only, and only to test hooks cheaply before committing. Not as an ad factory.

**4 · Budget the $3,000 as tuition, not investment.**

$3,000 ÷ $3.00 CPI = 1,000 installs × 2.9% install→paid × $35.64 year-1 LTV × 0.85 = **~$878 net**, recovered over twelve months. Even granting Apple its full conversion premium you land around $1,363. **Paid ads will not return your spend in year one at benchmark rates.** Judge this spend on whether it produced reliable unit economics — your real CPI, your real install→trial rate, which keyword intent converts — not on whether it produced profit. Those are the numbers you need before anyone puts $30,000 behind this.

And the free lever still dominates everything: hard paywall converts at **10.7%** vs freemium **2.1%**; 17–32 day trials convert at **42.5%** vs ≤4-day at **25.5%**. **Fix the paywall before you buy the traffic.**

## How to read this list

Almost everything below derives from **12 screen recordings and one brand kit.** The rest is resizing and templating. Do not treat this as 400 individual design jobs — it's about **three weeks of concentrated work**, front-loaded, then a VA filling templates.

Assets are tagged by month, which maps to the three product waves:

- **M1 (Sept)** — launch. Photo intake. *"Ten pieces. Fourteen outfits."*
- **M2 (Oct)** — voice ships. *"Talk for four minutes."*
- **M3 (Nov)** — the occasion + holiday. *"The wedding's in three weeks."*

⚠️ **Nothing in M1 may show voice intake.** It won't have shipped. Describing unshipped functionality in marketing is a Guideline 2.3.1 exposure.

## TIER 4 · Organic social — templates, not individual assets

The mistake here is designing 300 posts. Design **12 templates** and let a VA fill them.

### TikTok + Reels + Shorts — the main engine, 2/day × 2 accounts
- [ ] **6 video format templates**, 1080×1920:
  - The comparison (split-screen, `whering vs indyx` is a live search)
  - The math (`ten pieces, watch the number`)
  - Cost-per-wear reveal
  - The specific number (`I photographed 214 items, I wear 31`)
  - The comment magnet (divisive question)
  - The straight demo
- [ ] **90 hook text cards** — the only element that changes per post. Batch-written, batch-designed, dropped into the templates.
- [ ] 1 cover-frame template (Reels covers, 1080×1920, title-safe area marked)
- [ ] ⚠️ Batch 40–60 clips **before** launch. Posting daily from an empty bank fails in week two.

### Instagram
- [ ] **6 carousel templates**, 1080×1350 (portrait outperforms square on reach)
- [ ] **12 single-image templates**, 1080×1350
- [ ] **8 Story templates**, 1080×1920 — poll, question box, countdown, link, quote, before/after, stat, CTA
- [ ] 3 Highlight covers — canvas ground, ink icon: *How it works · Occasions · Questions*

### Pinterest — 3–5 fresh pins/day, 14-day median save→convert lag
- [ ] **8 pin templates, 1000×1500 (2:3)** — never exceed 1000×2100 or it truncates
- [ ] **6 board covers**, keyword-titled to mirror your ASO: *Capsule Wardrobe · Outfit Calendar · Packing List Outfits · What To Wear To Work · Wedding Guest Outfits · Closet Organisation*
- [ ] 2 Idea Pin templates, 1080×1920 (3.2× the outbound clicks of standard pins)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Never pin a bare App Store link** — Pinterest's in-app browser won't launch it, and a raw store link gives Pinterest zero on-site engagement signal, which suppresses your distribution. Pin to a landing page.

### X
- [ ] **12 image cards, 1600×900** — the stat, the quote, the number, the comparison
- [ ] 1 pinned-post graphic
- [ ] Video: repurpose 9:16 masters, no separate cut needed

### Reddit
- [ ] No design assets. Text and comment karma only. Standalone launch posts get you banned from the subs that matter — `r/SideProject` is the one permissive venue.
