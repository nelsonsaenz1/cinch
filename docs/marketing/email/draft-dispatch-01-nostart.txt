Ten pieces. That is the whole ask.

Hi {{{FIRST_NAME|there}}} — you made a Cinch account and the closet is still empty. Fair enough — most closet apps want your entire wardrobe before they do anything. This one wants ten things.

START HERE
Photograph ten things you already own.
Not the whole wardrobe. Ten. Cinch cuts the background out, works out what each piece is, and starts pairing them. Most people finish before their coffee does. If you only ever do one thing in this app, do this.

THEN
Watch the number.
When the tenth piece lands, Cinch counts every outfit those ten can make and shows you the total. It is almost always larger than people expect — that is the entire point of the app. After that, every morning the Today tab has an outfit picked for the weather where you actually are.

Doing it with a friend makes it faster and considerably more fun — you can see each other’s closets and borrow. Open Cinch, tap Invite, send your link.
