# LAYER 2 — the thesis

## 7. Tonight's thesis — *Cinch is a $20-a-day paid funnel with a good first ten minutes, no second day, and an ask placed before the payoff*

**What kind of business is Cinch tonight?** For the first time the answer can be given with an
attribution that separates an ad from a search, and it is uncomfortable in a different way from
every prior thesis. Cinch is not distribution-starved in the sense the 09-03 thesis meant — the
top of the funnel is steady at 5–6 installs a day. It is **entirely purchased**. Seven consecutive
days of Apple Ads installs match ASC's first-time downloads to within one; the organic residual
is two installs a week. The six-fold "surge" that killed the retention thesis on 09-06, the
"same searchers, more of them installing" read of 09-07, the "PPV→download jumped to 53%" of A47
— all of it was the EXACT campaign spending $11, $17.50 and $25 on three days. The 09-08 collapse
was the ad group's end date. Every acquisition claim since 09-05 is retracted in §17.1, and the
listing thesis (A4) with it: two thirds of the impressions it was scored on were ad impressions.

**Given that, what is the binding constraint?** Three candidates, and I will rank them rather than
name one, because the top-of-funnel number just changed *meaning* even though it did not change
size (METHODOLOGY, "do not name a binding constraint in a week the top-of-funnel moved").

*The faucet.* At $20/day, 40 installs → 27 real accounts → 4 ten-piece closets a week (this
week: more). If the campaign is off — and tonight's report says $0 — the business is at ~0.3
installs a day. That is not a constraint so much as a precondition: nothing downstream is
readable without it, which is why A67's kill rule exists.

*The second day.* 11 of 13 in the 09-05/06 cohort never returned after their first hour. The
09-06 "day-2 return" thesis was retracted on 09-07 because arrivals moved 6× with nothing shipped
— **and that retraction was itself wrong**, because the arrivals were paid. The retention finding
was never contradicted; the thing that seemed to contradict it was an ad budget. With the
attribution corrected, the return data stands: two tokens, zero sends, no widget, and a user who
builds ten and sees an outfit in ten minutes has nothing that brings her back on day two. This is
where four-for-four re-engagement pessimism lives, and it is now the thesis's centre.

*The ask.* 12 impressions, 0 purchases, n far below twenty — but the *placement* is a mechanism
claim, not a user claim, and it holds at n=4: every `ten_completed` ask is delivered before the
first outfit, by design. Tonight two strangers were asked for money 4 and 12 seconds before Cinch
showed them what it does. That is not a price question (A60) or a trial-button question (A12,
closed). It is an ordering question, one line of code, and it should be settled before any
further inference is drawn from the zero.

**The strongest case against.** First: n. Thirteen people in the retention cohort, four in the
ask sample, seven days of attribution. METHODOLOGY's twenty-people rule applies to the
retention half, and I mark it **provisional**. Second: the 09-05/06 cohort predates A59, A61b and
1.2.4; tonight's two Reveal-completers are a better product than the cohort saw. If `747c69f2`
(who came back after 33 minutes) opens the app tomorrow, that is one data point against.
Third — the honest one — *maybe organic is not zero, it is unmeasurable*: an install with
tracking consent withheld is invisible to Apple Ads and visible to ASC, which would push paid
below ASC, not above it; the match says opt-outs are rare, not that organic is. I accept that
the residual could be as high as ~10/week. It is not 30.

**What would change my mind.** (a) A67 reads $0 again tomorrow and ASC still shows 5+ downloads
— then there is an organic layer this file cannot see and the alert is wrong in scale. (b) ≥3 of
today's 8 arrivals open the app on 09-11 or later — then the second day is not the wall. (c) The
ask is moved after the Reveal and 20 impressions later there is still no trial — then it is
demand, and the price row (A60) becomes the product row.

**Is $50K-by-December still live? No.** With organic at ~0 and CPI at $3.35, 5,005 payers at
0% conversion is a paid-media plan without a conversion rate to multiply. **Is the 1 Nov verdict
still live? Yes, and it is now more honest than it was** — Condition 2 has its first real number
(organic ≈ 2/week) and Condition 1 has a real CPI beside it. Both are failing for measured
reasons instead of unmeasured ones.
